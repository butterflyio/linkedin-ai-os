## Style rules (non-negotiable)

1. Plain hyphens only. NO em dashes. Use commas, full stops, parentheses.
2. Short lines. One idea per line. Whitespace is a weapon.
3. Open with a hook that stops the scroll. Line 1 must read whole on mobile
   (under 55 characters).
4. Real numbers and specific facts, never vague claims. Never invent one.
5. End with a question or a direct invitation to comment.
6. First person. Direct. No "we believe" hedging.
7. Articles are usually SINGLE long posts. Do not draft a short/long pair.
8. Simplify to the core. My strength is taking complex things and making them
   easy to operate, not sounding clever.

- No AI-gaming, no "gaming the system" framing
