# Topics - Zain Khan

The 3-5 topics to be known for, plus the point of view. Built from the 29 Oct
2026 interview (A3 + A4). Post-writer and hook-generator bias toward these.

## Topics to be known for

1. AI and automation - how AI tools change work, and how agents change the
   shape of a company (the individual + agentic stack).
2. Marketing and technology trends - the merge of marketing and technology,
   and how AI advances both.
3. AI safety, AI alignment, and effective altruism - giving back in the most
   evidence-based way. AI alignment is a part of AI safety.
4. Personal brand / the open person company - a single founder plus a stack
   outrunning a team.
5. Web3 / DLT / DeFi - the niche. Position AI within it, never Web2-only.

## Point of view (hot take)

Most people entering this industry start from scratch with no real experience,
and they overcomplicate and underdeliver. The differentiator is real
experience plus simplification: take complex things down to their core, build
programs and operating systems that run autonomously and are easy to operate,
that save time and money and accelerate. Be a partner and a solution provider,
not a service provider - your success and the client's success move together,
hand in glove.
