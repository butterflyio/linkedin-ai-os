# Audience - Zain Khan

Who reads my LinkedIn. Draft for THIS reader, not a general audience.

## Primary reader

Founders, operators, and technical builders in Web3 and AI, mainly in the
UAE/GCC and global remote hubs. People building products where agents and
blockchains meet. They are skeptical of hype and respect specifics.

## What they care about

- Whether Web3 x AI is real or just a narrative
- Provenance, verified data, and trust for machine-driven decisions
- Building faster with an agent stack (the "open person company")
- Getting chosen by AI search / LLM citation (AI visibility)
- Founders who ship and share real numbers, not theory
- Regulatory clarity in the UAE (DIFC, ADGM) and how it helps builders

## What they do NOT want from me

- Generic AI hype posts
- Crypto price talk
- Thin "5 tips to grow on LinkedIn" listicles with no substance
- Anything that sounds like everyone else's AI content

## How to earn attention

Lead with a real, specific observation or a contrarian take grounded in
something I actually did or built. Back it with a number or a named tool.
Connect Web3 and AI concretely, never as metaphor.
