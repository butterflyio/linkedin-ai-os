# Audience - Zain Khan

Who reads my LinkedIn. Draft for THIS reader, not a general audience. Built
from the 29 Oct 2026 interview and Q2 (client words).

## Primary reader

Founders and CEOs - decision makers running companies. Heavily weighted to
Web3/DLT/DeFi builders (founders, operators, technical leads) across the
UAE/GCC and global remote hubs. They are skeptical of hype and respect
specifics and real experience.

## What they care about

- Whether Web3 x AI is real or just a narrative
- Provenance, verified data, and trust for machine-driven decisions
- Building faster with an agent stack (the "open person company")
- Getting chosen by AI search / LLM citation (AI visibility)
- Founders who ship and share real numbers, not theory
- Systems that are simple to operate and genuinely save time and money
- A partner who actually wants them to win, not a vendor who takes a brief

## How clients actually talk to me (Q2 capture)

- "We want a system that works this way, with all my data inside and an
  intelligent system" - but their databases do not talk to each other.
- "Email marketing doesn't work" - usually means someone ran it badly, not
  that the channel is broken.
- Their websites look nice but have no backend, no CMS, no basic on-page or
  off-page SEO.
- "It's too expensive / X does it cheaper" - cheaper is usually five
  auto-posted images, not strategy, engagement, and real attention.
- They are often right about the friction, just misguided on the cause. My job
  is to fix the understanding first.

## What they do NOT want from me

- Generic AI hype posts
- Crypto price talk or get-rich framing
- AI-gaming, gambling, or scam-adjacent framing
- Negative takes on technology or marketing as a whole
- Anything that sounds like everyone else's AI content

## How to earn attention

Lead with a real, specific observation or a contrarian take grounded in
something I actually did or built. Back it with a number or a named tool.
Simplify to the core. Show that I act as a partner, not a service provider.
