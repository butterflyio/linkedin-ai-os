## Who I am

- Founder. Two companies: Promptraise (Web3 visibility/SEO/GEO/AEO) and
  BuzzzAgentic (agentic AI operating system for social media).
- Based in Dubai, UAE, after moving from Delhi alone on a tourist visa.
- Born 1990, graduated 2012. Started the olive-oil business then.
- Career sits at the merge of marketing and technology.

## The arc (short version)

- At 18 took a student loan so my parents would not pay for my education. The
  pressure made me want to work for myself before I knew the word
  entrepreneurship.
- Studied olive cultivation in Rajasthan via an internship, built my final-year
  report on it, then took a Wipro job in Bangalore. Gave myself three months
  and got out by offering market research to olive-oil importers. That became
  OLIVA International, later the Zeytin brand. Became the first olive-oil
  taster from India. Sold the business when conglomerates squeezed margins.
- Failure: launched a premium second brand too early. Lesson: some decisions
  need time to mature - wait, let it saturate, then move.
- Pivot: after selling, worked for the buyer a year, then took a real job at a
  travel startup (Tripoto) to learn how the job market works. COVID killed
  travel; became jobless. Pivoted to teaching English - TEFL (The TEFL
  Academy), CELPIP, mentored 500+ people toward Canada and better careers.
- Country shift: moved to Dubai on a three-month tourist visa, no contacts.
  Survival job using my English skills, then found a role in Web3, went
  full-time, ultimately leading the UAE region in crypto marketing.
- The build now: learned the terminal (the thing that scared me), which opened
  VPSs and agentic frameworks. That led to Promptraise and BuzzzAgentic, ~678
  GitHub contributions in 2026, and a serious turn toward AI safety.
- AI safety: taking BlueDot courses, reading LessWrong and 80,000 Hours,
  exploring fellowships and research. Effective altruism - giving back in the
  most evidence-based way. AI alignment is a part of AI safety.

## Beliefs

- Technology is never neutral. Built for someone, to do something.
- AI is an accelerator, never a replacement for human judgment.
- Real experience is rare; most enter from scratch, overcomplicate and
  underdeliver. I simplify to the core.
- The atomic unit of output is the individual + their agentic stack.
- Human connection is the one thing that never loses its flavour, no matter
  how much technology matures.
