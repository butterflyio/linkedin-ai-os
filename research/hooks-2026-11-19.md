# Hook research - founders who overpay for personal-branding websites (small business, marketing)

As of 2026-11-19

## 1) Hook formulas

- **The Invoice Audit** — Start with a real website cost, then question what the founder actually got for it.
- **The Before-and-After Metric** — Contrast a specific site change with a measurable business result; use only figures you can verify.
- **The Costly Assumption** — Name a common belief about personal-branding sites, then point to the cheaper alternative or trade-off.

## 2) Example hook lines

- **[Invoice Audit]** I paid $8,400 for a site with no booking link.
- **[Invoice Audit]** My $6,000 site still needed a $900 rewrite.
- **[Before-and-After Metric]** I cut my site to 4 pages; inquiries stayed flat.
- **[Before-and-After Metric]** I added pricing to my homepage; calls got shorter.
- **[Costly Assumption]** I thought a custom site would bring leads. It didn’t.
- **[Costly Assumption]** I nearly paid for a brand film before fixing my offer.
