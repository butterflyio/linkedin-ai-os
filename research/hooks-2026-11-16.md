# Hook research - personal branding websites

As of 2026-11-16

I can’t verify LinkedIn posts from Nov. 9–16, 2026, so I can’t claim these are the top-performing hooks from the last seven days. These are testable formulas suited to personal branding websites—not a live performance ranking.

**1) Three hook formulas**

- **Specific Fix:** Name one concrete change to a personal site and what it clarifies.
- **Before → After:** Contrast a site’s old impression with the intended one.
- **Small Proof:** Share a specific result or observation, with no inflated claim.

**2) Eight example hooks**

- [Specific Fix] I moved my case studies above the fold.
- [Specific Fix] I rewrote my homepage for one kind of client.
- [Specific Fix] I added my rates to my portfolio site.
- [Before → After] My site used to read like a résumé.
- [Before → After] I replaced my tagline with a clear offer.
- [Before → After] I cut six menu links from my website.
- [Small Proof] One project page now shows my role and results.
- [Small Proof] I got better-fit inquiries after narrowing my site.
