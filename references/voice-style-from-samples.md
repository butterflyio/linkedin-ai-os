# Voice Profile - style drawn from borrowed samples

This profile is derived ONLY from the 4 sample posts (Dean D., Charlie Hills,
Connor Gillivan, Axelle Malek) the user chose to learn style from (Option B).
It is a STYLE reference, not the user's established voice. The authoritative
voice is brands/you/voice.md, built from the user's own interview. Patterns
merely absent from this small sample are labeled provisional with counts - do
not treat absence as a permanent ban.

## Who I sound like
A practical operator in the AI/marketing/Web3 space who explains things the
way they'd explain them to a colleague: concrete, honest about friction, and
slightly wry about predictable failures. Draws on specific recent experience
rather than theory. (Blend of the 4 samples; not the user's personal voice.)

## Tone
Hits:
- Pragmatic and direct (say what works, why, and the catch)
- Specific over vague (real numbers, named tools, real situations)
- Slightly wry / self-aware about predictable failures (3 of 4: "the irony
  writes itself", "nod along then hunt for the note", "useless Fn key")
- Confident but not boastful; results shown, not claimed as status

Never hits (from gaps):
- Never hype or breathless enthusiasm; none of the 4 oversells
- Never corporate-flat; none reads like a press release or a course blurb

## Sentence rhythm
Short to medium lines, one idea per line or short clause, broken for the
mobile feed. Sample 1 runs longer reflective sentences, samples 2 and 4 use
very short bursts, sample 3 is medium. Common floor: a sentence per line.
Avoidances observed: none of the samples use long unbroken paragraphs; all
break often. Provisional (4/4): no 25+ word wall-of-text sentences.

## Hook patterns
- The contrarian claim with a turn (4/4): "The hardest marketing briefs aren't
  tight deadlines... It's a product when no one knows why." / "Your AI
  automations break when you're not looking." / "Typing every prompt is the
  slow way to use AI."
- The opened-with-a-number (1/4, sample 3): "Most weeks I've got north of 20
  calls on the calendar."
- Absent across all 4: no hook opens with a rhetorical question, none opens
  "imagine a world where", none opens with a definition.

## How I open
Open with a concrete claim that names the problem and implies the fix, ideally
with a turn or a number to earn the scroll. Avoids (4/4): no question-hooks,
no "in today's world" scene-setting, no self-introduction in the open.

## How I close
Close with a usability or philosophy beat grounded in the post: sample 1 ends
on an aphorism ("you can't build a bridge when nobody told you where it needs
to go"), sample 2 on a repost CTA, sample 3 on results + a P.S. persona hook,
sample 4 on a witty observation. So the close is either a clean landing line or
a low-friction ask. Avoids (4/4): no "in conclusion" summaries, no motivational
pep-talk closers.

## Signature phrases
- "the irony writes itself" (sample 2)
- "So you..." second-person thread (sample 1)
- "Here's what I prefer about it" / "Here's the order I'd follow" (samples 4)
- "which means" hard-numbers clarification of a soft claim
- Backing a point with "me-too / parity / vision-led" type framing (sample 1)

## Off-limits
Distinguish PROHIBITION (from the user's interview, permanent) from PATTERNS
ABSENT in the 4 samples (provisional):

Permanent (user-set, from voice.md):
- No em dashes; plain hyphens, commas, full stops only
- No AI-gaming, gambling/bet, scam, negative tech/marketing takes,
  no "fully automated without human oversight"
- Single long post, no short/long pair

Provisional (absent in all 4 samples, count shown):
- No hashtags as content (2/4 use 1-2; Charlie used 2, Connor used 3)
- No bullet-callout "big takeaway in ALL CAPS" enders (0/4)
- No "question-only" posts (0/4)

## What this voice never does
Specific behaviors absent across the samples:
- Never opens with a rhetorical question (0/4)
- Never writes an unbroken wall-of-text paragraph (0/4)
- Never cites an unverifiable "experts say" claim (0/4)
- Never ends on a motivational summary or "in conclusion" (0/4)
- Never uses the "not X, but Y" construction to mass-produce a hook (0/4)
  (sample 1 does use one "not creative block... it's structural" - so label
  this as 1/4, present but not the dominant move)
