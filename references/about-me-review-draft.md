## Name and role
Zain Khan (@zk_uae). Founder. Two companies: Promptraise (Web3 visibility,
SEO, GEO and AEO for Web3 projects) and BuzzzAgentic (an agentic AI operating

## Audience
Founders and CEOs who run companies - the people making the decisions. Within
that, the core reader is a Web3/DLT/DeFi builder: an operator, technical lead,
or solo founder shipping a protocol or project across the UAE/GCC and global
remote hubs. They are skeptical of hype, respect real experience and specific
numbers, and want systems that are simple to operate and genuinely save time
and money.

## Topic pillars
- AI and automation: how AI tools change work, and how agents change the shape
  of a company (the individual + agentic stack).
- Marketing and technology: the merge of the two, and how AI advances both.
- AI safety, AI alignment, and effective altruism: giving back in the most
  evidence-based way. AI alignment is a part of AI safety.
- Personal brand and the open person company: a single founder plus a stack
  outrunning a team.
- Web3 / DLT / DeFi: the niche. AI positioning within it, never Web2-only.

## Point of view
Most people who enter this industry start from scratch with no real experience,
and they overcomplicate and underdeliver. The differentiators are real
experience and simplification: take complex things down to their core, build
systems that run autonomously and are easy to operate, save time and money and
accelerate. Be a partner and a solution provider, not a service provider -
the client's success and mine move together, hand in glove.
