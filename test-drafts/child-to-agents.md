A kid once wanted to build things.
Websites. Cool marketing. Big ideas.
He never could.
There were too many strings to pull.
Too many people to coordinate.
Too much he did not know.

I was that kid.

At 18 I started my first business.
Olive oil.
I bought it. Stored it. Distributed it.
It needed trucks.
It needed manual labour.
And it had a shelf life that never waited.
I knew nothing about tech back then.

What that business taught me was simple.
Starting matters.
Scaling matters more.

So I sold it.
And I started travelling.
I sat with olive growers in Italy.
I watched how the world actually runs.
One thing became clear.
My next business would be digital.
No trucks. No warehouse. No shelf life.

Then came the coaching business.
It solved the physical problem.
But it had a new one.
I was the product.
Every hour of growth was an hour of my life.
There was no multiplier.

Today things are different.
I run a few businesses.
And I finally build the things I dreamed about as a kid.
Because I do not build alone anymore.

I coordinate.
A small swarm of AI agents does the making.
I do the deciding.
Together we ship websites, marketing and systems that once needed a team.

I solve two problems.

One. Visibility across the internet.
SEO driven by AEO and GEO.
Built for the long term.
Google's E-E-A-T included.

Two. Social media that eats your whole day.
So I am building BuzzzAgentic.
An operating system that runs your whole social suite from one place.
Plus websites, LinkedIn marketing and the systems around it.

The kid who could not build now orchestrates.
That is the open person company.
One person. A real stack.
