By month three in Dubai, I was ready to quit.

I had moved from Delhi.

Three-month tourist visa.
No sponsorship.
No contacts.
Little money.

Back home I had everything.
A car, a house, my parents, my friends, a whole network.

In Dubai, all of that became my problem to solve.

Where to stay.
How to commute.
What to eat.
How to find work.

Lived off Sheikh Zayed Road.
Co-worked near Emirates Towers.
Went to almost every free event on Luma.
Gave interviews.

Nothing stuck.

By the last month I was close to giving up.

Then one small customer-service role opened up.
It leaned on my English.

Nothing you build ever goes to waste.

That role bought me breathing room.
And a visa.

Then I found a role in Web3.
There was no regulator yet.
The government was drawing builders in.

I ran two jobs.
Quit the traditional one.
Went full-time Web3.

Leading the UAE region in crypto marketing came next.

The reset was never the business idea.
It was learning to stand on my own in a place where nobody knew me.

If you have rebuilt your life in a new country, what did it teach you?
