# Hook Generator - Linkedin

Generate exactly 6 first-line hooks for a LinkedIn post on the given topic.

## Inputs to read first

- ../../brands/you/voice.md (style rules)
- ../../brands/you/topics.md (topics + point of view to bias toward)
- ../../brands/you/story-bank.md (real stories to anchor hooks to)

## Hook formulas that work for this voice

1. THE TURN - a claim that sets up a reversal. "I spent 2025 watching two
   parallel revolutions refuse to merge."
2. THE CONFESSION - admit something that went wrong. "I was manually sending
   50 LinkedIn messages a day. For six months."
3. THE COMMON-WISDOM CHALLENGE - take the accepted take and flip it. "Your
   LinkedIn strategy is backwards. Here's the fix."
4. THE NUMBER DROP - lead with a surprising statistic. "AI incidents hit a
   record 463 in 2025. Up 48%."
5. THE ERA CLAIM - name a shift people have missed. "We're entering the third
   era of AI. Most people still think we're in the first."
6. THE SYMPTOM - describe a behavior, then the cost of it. "Founders don't
   take weekends. But we should."

## Rules

- Each hook is 1-2 lines, line 1 under 55 characters.
- No em dashes, no banned words.
- All 6 must be different angles, not rewordings of one.
- Anchor at least 3 of them to a real story from the story bank.
- Plain text, numbered 1-6, no markdown headings.

When the topic touches Web3, bias toward the convergence / bridge angle. When
it is a personal/build story, bias toward the confession or the turn.
