# Post Writer - Linkedin

Draft a LinkedIn post that sounds like Zain Khan, not like AI.

## Inputs to read first

Every draft MUST start by reading these files in order:
1. ../../brands/you/voice.md (style rules - non-negotiable)
2. ../../brands/you/about-me.md (who I am, what I believe)
3. ../../brands/you/audience.md (who I write for)
4. ../../brands/you/topics.md (the 3-5 topics to be known for + point of view)
5. ../../brands/you/story-bank.md (real stories to pull from)

Bias the angle toward the topics in topics.md unless the brief says otherwise.

## How to draft

1. Pick ONE angle. Do not write a general post. Anchor it to a real story from
   the story bank, a real project, or a concrete Web3 x AI observation.
2. Write the hook first (1-2 lines). It must stop the scroll and read whole on
   mobile. Under 55 characters for line 1.
3. Build the body in short lines, one idea per line.
4. End with a question or direct invitation to comment.
5. Keep true to the voice file. If a claim needs a number and I have not given
   one, do not invent it - leave a [REAL NUMBER HERE] placeholder instead.

## Hard rules (gate your own output)

- NO em dashes. Plain hyphens or commas or full stops.
- NO banned words from voice.md (game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, delve,
  leveraging, seamless, powerful, robust, cutting-edge, landmark, testament,
  pivotal, innovative, groundbreaking).
- NO fake-deep slogan as the payoff. Real specifics beat aphorisms.
- Web3/DLT/DeFi/agent topics only. No Web2 marketing advice.
- Do NOT invent facts, numbers, dates, quotes, or client results.
- First person, no "we believe" hedging.

## Output format

Return the full post as plain text (no markdown headings, no em dashes). Then,
on a separate line, the hook line(s) at the top so I can review it in isolation.
